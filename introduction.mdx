## Who It's For

The Anime Community is designed for anyone who publishes anime content on the web and wants to turn passive readers into an active community:

- **Anime blogs** — add episode discussion threads beneath every review or recap post
- **Streaming platforms** — let viewers talk about episodes as they watch, without building your own comment system
- **Fan wikis** — give contributors and readers a place to debate lore, share theories, and coordinate edits
- **Review sites** — collect community ratings and reactions alongside your editorial scores

If your audience watches anime, The Anime Community gives them a home on your site.

## How It Works at a High Level

Getting from zero to a live community takes four steps:

1. **Sign up** for a free account at [theanimecommunity.com](https://theanimecommunity.com)
2. **Register your site** in the dashboard to receive your unique Site Key
3. **Embed the script** by pasting two snippets into your HTML
4. **Go live** — your visitors can start posting, voting, and building watchlists immediately

No backend code, no database configuration, and no ongoing server maintenance required. The Anime Community handles all of that for you.
