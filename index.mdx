## Get Started in 3 Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up at [theanimecommunity.com](https://theanimecommunity.com) and register your site to get your unique site key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the embed script">
    Paste a single `<script>` tag into your site's HTML. No backend or database needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customize and go live">
    Configure colors, features, and moderation settings from your dashboard, then publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Already have an account? Head to the [Quick Start guide](/quickstart) to add the widget to your site right now.
</Note>
